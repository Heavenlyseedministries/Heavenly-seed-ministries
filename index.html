About Us

Heavenly Seed Ministries is a faith-based mission dedicated to serving people through love, compassion, and the message of Jesus Christ. We believe in planting seeds of hope in communities through service, care, and spiritual growth.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

To spread the love of Christ and transform lives through faith, compassion, and community service.

Mission

To support vulnerable communities through spiritual guidance, care for the elderly, education for children, and support for widows and families in need.

Our Services

Old Age Home Care

Providing love, shelter, and support for elderly people.

Children Hostel

Supporting children with education, care, and a safe place to grow.

Education Support

Helping children receive quality education and guidance.

Widow Support

Caring for widows and providing assistance for daily needs.

Medical Help

Helping families with hospital bills and healthcare support.
