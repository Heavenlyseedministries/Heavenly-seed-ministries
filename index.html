About Us

Heavenly Seed Ministries is a faith-based organization dedicated to serving communities with love and compassion. Our mission is to support the elderly, widows, and children through care, education, and spiritual guidance.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To spread the love of Christ and transform lives through faith, compassion, and community service.

Mission

To serve vulnerable communities through education, care programs, and the message of hope.

Our Services

Old Age Care

Providing shelter, care, and love to elderly people.

Children Hostel

Supporting children with education and safe housing.

Education Support

Helping children receive proper education.

Widow Support

Helping widows with support and daily needs.

Medical Assistance

Helping families with hospital bills and medical care.
