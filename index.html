About Heavenly Seed Ministries

Heavenly Seed Ministries is a Christian faith-based ministry dedicated to serving communities with love, compassion, and the message of Jesus Christ. Our mission is to care for elderly people, support widows, educate children, help tribal communities, and bring hope to families in need.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To spread the love of Christ and transform communities through faith, compassion, and service.

Mission

To support vulnerable communities through education, food distribution, spiritual guidance, and compassionate care.

Ministry Programs

Old Age Care

Providing love, care, and support for elderly people.

Children Hostel

Providing safe housing and education support for children.

Education Support

Helping children and tribal communities receive education.

Widow Support

Helping widows with financial and emotional support.

Medical Assistance

Helping families with hospital bills and medical care.

Food Distribution

Distributing rice bags and groceries to poor families.

Women Empowerment

Encouraging women through education and development programs.

Evangelism Outreach

Sharing the Good News of Jesus Christ in communities.

Bible Training

Teaching biblical principles and spiritual growth.

Mission Work

Serving rural and tribal communities through mission outreach.

Church Support

Supporting local churches and ministry activities.
