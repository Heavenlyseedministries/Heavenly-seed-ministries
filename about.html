About Heavenly Seed Ministries

Heavenly Seed Ministries is committed to serving the poor, widows, elderly people, and children in need. Our mission is to spread love, compassion, and the message of hope through practical support and ministry work.

We provide care for elderly people without family support, education and shelter for tribal children, support for widows, and assistance for families in need.
