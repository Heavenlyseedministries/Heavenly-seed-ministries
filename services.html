Our Ministry Services

Old Age Home Care

Caring for elderly people without family support, providing food, shelter, and love.

Children's Hostel

Providing safe accommodation and education support for tribal and poor children.

Widow Support

Helping widows with financial help, care, and emotional support.

Medical Assistance

Providing medical help and treatment support for poor families.

Food Distribution

Distributing food and groceries to poor families and needy people.

Bible Training

Teaching the Word of God and training believers for ministry.

Mission Work

Evangelism and outreach programs to share the Gospel.

Education Support

Helping poor children with school supplies and education support.

Disaster Relief

Helping families affected by floods, disasters, and emergencies.

Skill Development Program

Training youth and women with skills to become self-reliant.
